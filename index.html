FAQ

Questions.

Can you guarantee views?

No one honestly can, the algorithm decides. What we guarantee: your track as the sound, the artist credited, a preview for your approval, the video published on the agreed page, and full stats after 36–48 hours.

How do I know the pages are yours?

Send us any code, like 4521-8890, and we'll put it in the bio of the page you choose for 24 hours.

Which tracks work best?

Tracks with a strong beat and energy from the first seconds: phonk, Brazilian funk, house, slap and bass house, remixes, slowed and sped-up versions, melodic trap. Not sure about yours? Send it and we'll tell you honestly.

How fast do you deliver?

First previews within 24–48 hours after payment. A 3-video package is usually live within a few days.

How do I pay?

Crypto (USDT or USDC), direct bank transfer in USD, or PayPal for packages. Payment comes before the edit, and you approve every preview before anything is posted.

Can I spread a package across several tracks?

Yes. Labels often use a 3-video package for three different releases to see which one connects best.
