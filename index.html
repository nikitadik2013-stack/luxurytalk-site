FAQ

Questions

Do you pay me for my track?

No, it's the other way around. This is a paid promotion service for artists: we feature your track as the sound in our videos and credit you, so new listeners discover your music.

Can you guarantee views?

No one can honestly guarantee views on TikTok, because the algorithm decides. We guarantee a quality edit, the agreed page and timing, and full stats. Our best video reached 6.7M views.

What music works best?

Tracks with a strong beat or a memorable hook: hip-hop, phonk, electronic, house, pop and instrumentals. Tracks without explicit Russian lyrics work best for our international audience.

Where is the audience from?

Roughly 15–20% USA, 20–30% Europe, and the rest mainly Latin America and CIS countries.

How do I pay?

Payment is upfront in crypto (USDT/USDC). If you don't use crypto, we'll guide you step by step through buying USDT with a regular card.
